*Required · Design*

Identify relevant artifacts from the human-centered design process that address the following points.

1. What techniques did you employ to discover users’ needs? 
- interviewed people (asked about letterboxd, general dhall-related needs, and about the features we were considering for the app)
- developed personas (three archetypes for three main types of users who would want to use the app)
- made prototypes and tested them with real users (I made a paper prototype of full functionality of all pages on the app and tested it on one of the people I interviewed before)
- post-prototype test interview for each person we tested on (what changes would they want? what was easy/difficult to use? etc.)
2. What did you learn about users’ needs from this process that you did not know beforehand?
- There are different types of people who would benefit from this app, including those who really want the social aspect and those who want to see the overall ratings to see if they even want to go to dhall that day/meal. The naming and organization of each widget and page in the app can significantly impact how users interact with it based on their expectations. Signifiers should be unique enough that it is clear what the goal of a feature is.
