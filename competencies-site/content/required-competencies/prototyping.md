*Required · Design*

Identify relevant artifacts from the human-centered design process that address the following points.

1. What design questions did you intend to answer with your prototype(s)?
    1. How easy and intuitive is it to review or log an item? 
    2. How easy and intuitive is it to find the information the user wants? 
    3. Does each page show what the user expects it to?
2. How did you design your prototype(s) and subsequent user studies to address these questions?
- I put every feature I could think of that may end up in the app into my paper prototype. I had users navigate it how they would use the app first to answer the second question. Then I asked them to try logging an item to answer the first question. Finally, I had them fully explore each page to answer the third question. 
3. What answers to your design questions did your efforts unveil?
- The design rips off letterboxd too much, but is intuitive. Each tab works as expected, although the friends tab is confusing as to what it would be. Most of the features seem useful.
