*Required · Design*

Identify relevant artifacts from the human-centered design process that address the following points.

1. What techniques did you use to discover solutions to users’ needs?
- First, we brainstormed: world's worst idea first, then talked to someone outside of our group for advantages and disadvantages general, then stream of consciousness to brainstorm interview questions. Then, we interviewed people before and after making prototypes. Then, we made personas: 
    1. Top Rater Ryan: priority is the social aspect and seeing friends' ratings (also the most active contributor type)
    2. Oldschool Ollie: priority is determining when to go to dhall
    3. Healthy Harlow: priority is supporting health through ratings, comments, recipes, and nutrition info on the app

2. From ideation, what are the must have features/components/etc. of your project? Why are they must haves?
- A better version of nutrislice's setup for how the information is organized on the menu is crucial because it will entice users who don't care about the social side to also use the app.
- Ratings are necessary because all user types will benefit most from this.
- Having some degree of anonymization without sacrificing all identifiability will ensure users who want to keep their ratings private and those who really enjoy the friend connection component can use the app freely. 
- Security: only allow users to sign up with grinnell emails to avoid getting unwanted users.
- Nice to have: the ideas of recipes and user-uploaded photos were brought up by multiple people we interviewed
